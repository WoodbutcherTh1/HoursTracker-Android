# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

This policy explains what HoursTracker ("we") collects, why, where it is kept, and your rights under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981. By using the app you agree to this policy.

## Data on your device

Your shifts, notes, workplace settings and marked days are kept only on this phone, in the app's private storage, and Android does not copy them to Google Drive or any other backup. The app also keeps an activity log of what changed on the phone (never amounts, times or notes). Your national ID number is kept encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore and is never sent anywhere. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

## Account and cloud backup

This version works without an account and sends none of your data to us. An optional cloud backup is planned. It will stay off until you turn it on, and when you do, the app will explain exactly what is backed up (never your national ID number) and where it is stored, and will ask you to agree again. You will be able to delete the backup at any time.

## Support messages and announcements

When you write to support, we receive your message, the email address you give us and, only if you choose to send it, details about your phone and the app version. A future version may register a random install ID, a notification token, and the app language and version so we can send you announcements, but never your shifts, pay, name or ID number.

## Tracking & ads

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

## How long we keep data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Shifts you delete from History are removed right away (you can undo for a few seconds). You can choose to delete shifts automatically after 1 to 10 years. The activity log is kept for one year unless you choose another period; its consent and deletion lines hold no personal data and are kept longer as proof. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

## Your rights

Under Israeli law you may see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We hold none of your shifts or pay: they exist only on your phone, where you can export, edit or delete them yourself. For anything else, write to us at info.hourstracker@gmail.com and we will answer as the law requires. You may also complain to the Privacy Protection Authority.

## Security

Data on your phone sits in the app's private storage, which Android encrypts when your phone has a screen lock. Your national ID number is additionally encrypted with a key that never leaves the Android Keystore. No system is perfectly secure; if a serious security incident affects your data, we will act as the law requires.

## Your controls

In Settings you can edit your details, turn reminders off, delete shifts (one by one, several at once, or automatically after 1 to 10 years), export your shifts as CSV or PDF, look through the activity log, download all your data as a JSON file (everything except your ID number), and delete all your data from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

## Minors

If you are under 18, use HoursTracker with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a meaningful way, the app will show you the new version and ask you to agree again before you continue.

## Contact

The data controller is Hmam Kaadna, the developer of HoursTracker. For any privacy question or request, write to info.hourstracker@gmail.com.
